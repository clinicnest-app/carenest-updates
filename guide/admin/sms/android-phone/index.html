Option 2: an Android phone of the clinic

One Android phone of the clinic sends the SMS. ClinicNest hands each message to an app on that phone, and the app sends it from the phone’s SIM. No registration and no SMS provider are needed.

What you need

Section titled “What you need”
  • An Android phone that stays in the clinic, with a SIM and an SMS pack, on the clinic’s Wi-Fi.
  • The phone switched on and charged whenever reminders are to go out.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”

On the phone

Section titled “On the phone”
  1. Open the Play Store and install Traccar SMS Gateway, by Traccar. Several apps have similar names; check the maker.
  2. Open the app. It asks to become the phone’s SMS app. Allow it.
  3. In the app’s gateway settings, switch on the local service. The app now shows an address (endpoint) and a token for the local service, and a long cloud token.

On the clinic computer

Section titled “On the clinic computer”

  1. Open Settings → SMS and tick Enable SMS sending.

  2. The page shows Provider: not set. Press Add and choose Android phone (no registration).

  3. Press Enter them on the phone. A QR code appears.

On the phone again

Section titled “On the phone again”
  1. Point the phone’s camera at the QR code. A page SMS from this phone opens.
  2. In the app, copy the local service’s address and paste it into Address; copy its token into Local token.
  3. Copy the cloud token into Cloud token.
  4. Press Send to ClinicNest. The page says ClinicNest has them.

Back on the clinic computer

Section titled “Back on the clinic computer”

  1. The dialog says Received from the phone. Press Done.

  2. The page now shows Provider: Android phone (no registration), with the local service and the cloud token, and Saved. Nothing has to be saved by hand.

  3. Under Send a test message, on the page or in the dialog, type your own mobile number and press Send test SMS.

The test SMS should arrive within a minute, from the number of the clinic’s phone. The screen says which way it went: “The phone, in the clinic’s network, accepted the message”.

From now on the reminders list shows Send SMS in place of SMS. To have the reminders go out by themselves, see Send reminders automatically.

The tokens can also be typed on the clinic computer, into the fields of the same dialog; the phone’s page only saves the typing, since the tokens are long.

Two ways to the phone

Section titled “Two ways to the phone”
Local service Cloud token
The way of a message ClinicNest → the phone, over the clinic’s Wi-Fi ClinicNest → Traccar’s server → Google → the phone
Number and text leave the clinic No Yes, on the way to the phone
Needs Phone and clinic computer on the same Wi-Fi Internet on both
A clinic whose ClinicNest is hosted on the internet Cannot use it Uses it

With both entered, ClinicNest tries the local service first. Only when the phone cannot be reached there – it is away, switched off, or the Wi-Fi gave it another address – does the message go through the cloud token. Either one alone works too.

The phone’s address can change

Section titled “The phone’s address can change”

The address of the local service, for example http://192.168.1.23:8082, is given to the phone by the Wi-Fi router. It usually stays the same for days or weeks, and can change after the phone was away or the router was restarted. Messages then go through the cloud token, or fail if there is none.

  • To keep it from changing: in the router’s settings, reserve the address for the phone (“DHCP reservation” or “static lease”).
  • After a change: press Edit, then Enter them on the phone again, and paste the new address.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • The phone must stay on. While it is off or out of charge, no SMS goes out.
  • About 100 SMS a day. In India most SIM plans include about that many. ClinicNest does not count them.
  • The app takes over SMS on that phone. Ordinary SMS on the phone are then read and written in this app.
  • “Accepted” means handed to the app. ClinicNest cannot tell whether the SMS reached the patient.
  • The patient sees the phone’s number, not the clinic’s name. Replies and calls come to that phone.
  • Anyone with a token can send SMS from that phone. Do not mail the tokens or post them anywhere.
  • A few reminders a day, not bulk. The rules for business SMS are written for registered senders. An operator can restrict or disconnect a SIM that sends many similar messages or that patients complain about. For more than about 100 SMS a day, use option 3.

If the test SMS does not arrive

Section titled “If the test SMS does not arrive”
  • “The phone could not be reached in the clinic’s network”: is the phone on the clinic’s Wi-Fi, is the app’s local service switched on, and is the address still the one the app shows?
  • An answer that mentions the token or “unauthorized”: the token is not the right one for that way. Enter them again from the phone.
  • The QR code’s page does not open on the phone: the phone is not on the clinic’s Wi-Fi, or the code is older than 15 minutes. Press Enter them on the phone for a new one.
  • The SIM: send an ordinary SMS from the phone to see whether its SMS pack still works.

To stop using the phone

Section titled “To stop using the phone”

Press Delete beside the provider, twice. ClinicNest forgets the tokens and switches SMS sending off; the reminder text stays.

The request itself

Section titled “The request itself”

Advanced: the request sent to the phone, at the foot of the set-up dialog, shows the web request ClinicNest sends. It is filled in by the choice of the Android phone and normally needs no change.
