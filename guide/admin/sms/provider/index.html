Option 3: an SMS provider (DLT)

The clinic opens its own account with an SMS provider and registers itself, its sender name and the reminder’s wording on a DLT portal. ClinicNest then sends the reminders through that account. The patient sees the clinic’s sender name in place of a phone number.

ClinicNest has no shared SMS account. The registration and the provider account belong to the clinic.

What you need

Section titled “What you need”
  • The clinic’s PAN and a proof of address of the business, such as a GST registration, a Shop Act licence or a certificate of incorporation.
  • About ₹5,900 for the one-time DLT registration fee, and money for SMS credits with the provider. Check the current fee on the portal.
  • A few days. The registration and the template are each approved by the operator, not at once.
  • Internet on the computer that runs ClinicNest.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”

1. Open an account with an SMS provider

Section titled “1. Open an account with an SMS provider”

ClinicNest has ready-made settings for MSG91 and Fast2SMS; any other provider that takes a web request works through Other provider. Open an account and complete the provider’s own verification.

2. Register the clinic on a DLT portal

Section titled “2. Register the clinic on a DLT portal”

Register once, on one operator’s portal; the registration is valid with all operators.

Operator Portal
Jio trueconnect.jio.com
Airtel dltconnect.airtel.in
Vi vilpower.in
BSNL ucc-bsnl.co.in
  1. Choose to register as an entity (the portals also call it “principal entity” or “enterprise”), not as a telemarketer.
  2. Enter the clinic’s details, upload the PAN and the proof of address, and pay the fee.
  3. Wait for approval. The portal then shows the clinic’s entity ID, a long number. Note it.

3. Register the sender name

Section titled “3. Register the sender name”

The sender name, called the header, is what the patient sees in place of a phone number.

  1. On the portal, add a header of the kind for service or transactional messages, not promotional.
  2. Choose six letters that stand for the clinic, for example CRNEST.
  3. Wait for approval.

4. Register the reminder’s wording

Section titled “4. Register the reminder’s wording”

Every message must match a registered content template.

  1. On the portal, add a content template of the type Service Implicit.

Note the order of the changing parts. In the text above it is: patient’s name, clinic, date, clinic’s phone. You need this order again in step 6.

5. Connect the provider

Section titled “5. Connect the provider”
  1. On the DLT portal, add your SMS provider as your telemarketer. The portals call this “PE–TM binding” or “chain”. The provider tells you its name or ID for this.
  2. In the provider’s account, enter the entity ID, the header and the template ID. Some providers then give the template an ID of their own; MSG91 does. Note that one too.
  3. In the provider’s account, create an API key (MSG91 calls it “authkey”).

6. Enter the details in ClinicNest

Section titled “6. Enter the details in ClinicNest”

  1. Open Settings → SMS.

  2. Tick Send SMS from ClinicNest.

  3. Under Provider, choose your provider, or Other provider.

  4. Fill in:

    Field What goes in
    API key The key from the provider’s account
    Sender ID The six-letter header
    DLT entity ID The entity ID, if your provider asks for it
    Reminder template ID The DLT template ID, or the provider’s own ID for the template
    Reminder text The approved wording, with {name}, {clinic}, {date} and {clinicPhone} in place of each {#var#}

  5. Under Request sent to the provider, check that the changing parts are sent in the same order as in your template. The ready-made settings for MSG91 and Fast2SMS send three parts in the order name, date, clinic and do not send the clinic’s phone. Either register your template with the parts in that order, or change the request to match your template.

  6. Press Save.

  7. Under Send a test message, type your own mobile number and press Send test SMS.

The test SMS should arrive from the clinic’s sender name. From now on the reminders list shows Send SMS in place of SMS. To have the reminders go out by themselves, see Send reminders automatically.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • Registration first. Nothing can be sent until the entity, the header and the template are all approved.
  • The wording is fixed. A message that differs from the approved template is dropped by the operator. To change the wording, register a new template and wait for its approval.
  • Each changing part has a maximum length on the DLT portals, 30 characters at the time of writing. A long clinic name may not fit; write the clinic’s name into the template’s fixed text in that case.
  • A charge per SMS, set by the provider, on top of the one-time fee: about 15 to 25 paise at the time of writing. A reminder longer than one SMS (160 characters in English, 70 in other scripts) is charged as two or more.
  • “Accepted” is not “delivered”. ClinicNest shows that the provider accepted the message. Whether it reached the patient is shown in the provider’s own delivery report.
  • The ready-made settings are starting points. Providers change their web requests; compare the request with your provider’s documentation.
  • Internet is needed on the computer that runs ClinicNest.

If the test SMS does not arrive

Section titled “If the test SMS does not arrive”
  • Read the answer shown under the test. ClinicNest shows what the provider answered, and providers usually name the problem: a wrong key, an unknown template, no credits.
  • “Accepted” but nothing arrives: the text does not match the approved template, or the parts are in the wrong order. Compare the text in the provider’s delivery report with the template, character by character.
  • The template is still waiting for approval on the DLT portal, or the provider is not yet added as your telemarketer.
