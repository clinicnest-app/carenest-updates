ClinicNest – free clinic management software for small clinics

Community Edition · free

Free clinic management software for small clinics: patient records, OPD, prescriptions and follow-ups. It runs on the clinic's own computer, works without internet, and keeps the data in the clinic.

Installation guide Support this project

Windows 10 / 11 · Mac with Apple silicon or Intel · Linux desktop

What the Community Edition includes

  • PatientsRegistration, quick search by name, phone or ID, and each patient's visits and results over time.
  • OPD queueToday's patients with daily token numbers: waiting, seen, and scheduled follow-ups.
  • Specialty formsEye clinic and ENT clinic forms (examination, refraction, audiometry, procedure notes), plus vitals and visit notes.
  • Diagnosis and prescriptionICD-10 diagnoses, a medicine list with usual doses, and printed prescriptions on your letterhead.
  • Photos and documentsTake photos with the phone's camera during the visit; add reports and old prescriptions as PDF.
  • Follow-ups and remindersNext-visit dates, a calendar, and day-before reminders by call, SMS or WhatsApp through the clinic's own provider account.
  • Charges and receiptsFees, discounts, payments by cash, UPI or card, printed receipts and the day's collection.
  • Whole clinic, one computerInstalled on one PC; reception, consulting room and phones open it in the browser. Only devices the doctor approved get in.
  • Your data stays with youDaily automatic backups, encrypted with your backup password, and no cloud account.
